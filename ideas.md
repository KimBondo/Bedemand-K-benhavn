<idea>
**Design Movement:** Nordic Quietism — Scandinavian minimalism with a funerary gravitas
**Core Principles:**
1. Absolute typographic hierarchy: Lora serif for headings, Open Sans for body, no decorative flourishes
2. Spatial generosity: 80px vertical sections, 40px column gaps — whitespace as emotional relief
3. Strict palette restraint: warm off-white (#F9F8F6), deep charcoal (#2F3E46), muted sage green (#84A98C), parchment divider (#DDD8D0)
4. Symmetrical 50/50 column layout — neither director dominates

**Color Philosophy:** Warm sand replaces clinical white; deep charcoal replaces harsh black. The muted sage green is the only active color — it signals safety and nature without urgency. Every tone is chosen to reduce cortisol, not stimulate it.

**Layout Paradigm:** Two-section vertical stack. Hero occupies 100vh. Distribution portal is a perfect 50/50 split. No sidebar, no grid complexity. The structure is a quiet corridor.

**Signature Elements:**
1. A 1px solid #DDD8D0 horizontal rule separating the contact block from the biography
2. Full-width green primary button — the single most prominent interactive element on the page

**Interaction Philosophy:** Zero page-load animations. Zero parallax. One permitted interaction: a 200ms ease-out background color shift on button hover. Stillness is the message.

**Animation:** No entrance animations. No scroll triggers. The page loads complete and still — like a room that has been prepared and is waiting.

**Typography System:** Lora 600 for H1/H2/H3 (letter-spacing 0.02em), Open Sans 400 for body (line-height 1.75), Open Sans 600 for button labels. Minimum body size 15px.
</idea>

## Selected Approach: Nordic Quietism

The first approach — **Nordic Quietism** — is the most faithful to the blueprint and the emotional needs of the user. It is chosen for its strict adherence to the design system specified in the blueprint, its symmetrical 50/50 layout, and its absolute stillness policy.

Every design decision will be tested against this question: **"Does this reduce or increase the cognitive load of a grieving user?"**
